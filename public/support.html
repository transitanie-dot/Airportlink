Frequently asked questions

How do I book a ride?

Visit our homepage, enter your pickup and dropoff locations, select date and time, then complete your booking with payment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through Stripe. Payment is processed securely online.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes, you can change or cancel your booking up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup. Contact support for assistance.

How do I view my past trips?

Go to "My trips" in your account. Your past trips appear in the "Past trips" section.

What if my driver doesn't show up?

Contact support immediately via email or chat. We'll help you resolve the situation quickly.
